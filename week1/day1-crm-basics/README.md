# Day 1 - CRM Basics

## 1. What is CRM?

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.

It is a system used by companies to store customer information, manage communication, track sales, and improve customer relationships.

CRM helps businesses:
- Organize customer data
- Track customer interactions
- Improve sales process
- Provide better customer service

Example:
A college can use CRM to manage student admissions and communication.

---

# 2. Why Companies Use Salesforce

Salesforce is a popular CRM platform used by companies because it is cloud-based and easy to use.

Companies use Salesforce to:
- Store customer data safely
- Track sales and leads
- Automate business tasks
- Improve communication
- Access data from anywhere

Benefits:
- Saves time
- Improves productivity
- Better customer management

---

# 3. Explain

## Account

An Account represents a company, organization, or customer.

Example:
ABC College

It stores details like:
- Name
- Address
- Phone number

---

## Contact

A Contact represents a person related to an account.

Example:
Student or faculty member of ABC College

It stores:
- Name
- Email
- Phone number

---

## Opportunity

An Opportunity represents a potential business deal or chance of sales.

Example:
A student applying for admission to a course.

It helps track:
- Admission status
- Fees
- Course interest
